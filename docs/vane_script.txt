Good evening. You're our only guest tonight. The house prefers it that way.

The front door is locked now. It has six locks, and every room in this house holds one of the keys.

Pass a room's trial and the house will give you its key. Fail it three times and the house will keep you instead.

One rule. No cameras. The house doesn't like to be looked at twice.

Your first trial is here in the hall: the clock. I won't be far.

The Doll Room. Every one of them was somebody's. Mind Marigold, in the cabinet at the back.

Your trial: when one of the little chairs is empty, Button is hiding. Find her before she stops giggling. The others do not like it when you lose.

The Card Parlour. Edgar Hollowmere played at that table every night for forty years, and never once lost.

Your trial: his chair is on the far side of the table, where the light falls. Sit in it and play him at higher or lower. Win four hands before you lose three.

The Antiquities Study. The oldest pieces in the collection, and the most dangerous.

Your trial: read the card on every relic in the red cases, then answer the ledger on my desk. Three poor appraisals, and the idol will want a word.

The Portrait Gallery. Every face on these walls once stood where you are standing.

Your trial: look away, and one of them will change. Find which one and examine it. Catch three of them at it.

The Passage. The last trial. Door II, at the end, will knock to you. Knock back exactly what you hear.

And the door marked Staff Only is not for guests. It is not for anyone.

I did say staff only.

I told you. Staff only. Now the door behind you is locked, and it will stay locked.

That lift goes down. Only down. No one who has stepped into it has ever come back up to tell me how far.

I said no cameras.

It has seen you now. It will follow you through this house, and it does not like to be looked at.

Six trials. Six keys. And not a single photograph.

The house has no claim on you. Go home, and do not come back.

Six trials, six keys. But you took pictures, after I asked you not to.

You may go. Whatever is in those photographs will follow you home.
