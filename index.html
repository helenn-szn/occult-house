OCCULT HOUSE
Built for the Manycore Aholo Global 3D AI Hackathon.

Rendering: three.js and Spark. All names, objects and characters are invented.
HOW TO PLAY · 1 OF 6

Six rooms. Six keys.

You are locked in a house of haunted things.

Every room holds a trial. Pass it to win a key and unlock the next room.

Bring all six keys to the front door to get out.
